01 Introduction

Classroom ("the app") is a classroom-management app for teachers. This policy explains what the free version of the app does with information. The short version: everything the teacher enters stays on the teacher's own device. The developer does not collect it, receive it, or have any way to access it.

The app currently offers only its free feature set. Paid features are not available at this time, and this policy will be updated before any are released.

02 Information the app stores

The app stores the records a teacher creates: the class name and division, student names, roll numbers, optional contact numbers and photos, attendance records, the weekly class schedule, and app preferences.

This information is written to the app's private storage on the device. It is not uploaded to the developer or to any server operated by the developer. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.

03 Collection, sharing and tracking

  • The developer does not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • The app has no internet permission. It cannot send data anywhere on its own.

04 Sharing from the app

When a teacher taps Share Report, the app creates an image of that student's report and hands it to Android's standard share sheet, where the teacher picks the app to send it with (for example WhatsApp). The teacher chooses the recipient and sends it. Nothing is sent automatically.

The image is written to the app's cache first and is removed by Android's normal cache cleanup. The privacy policy of whichever app receives the image applies to it.

05 Permissions

  • Run after restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): lets Android restore the app's scheduled maintenance after the device restarts.
  • The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS or broad file-access permissions. Student photos are chosen through Android's system photo picker, which only gives the app the single image the teacher selects.

06 Student data and responsibility

The app is intended for teachers and school staff. Student information is entered and controlled by the teacher. The teacher (or their school) is responsible for having the appropriate permission to store that information and for keeping the device secure.

The app is not directed at children, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

07 Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until the teacher deletes it in the app, deletes the class it belongs to, or uninstalls the app. Uninstalling removes the app's private data.

08 Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example, if paid features or cloud sync are added), this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the effective date above will change.
